# Khan video study — what Sal does on the board, and what `khan` does instead

Date: 2026-10-13. Method: six Sal-narrated videos watched on YouTube through the Claude-in-Chrome extension (no
sign-in, no banners needed; YouTube did not block the player). For each video, frames were captured at 10 %…90 %
of its length plus two bursts of 1 s-apart frames (limits 0:28–0:46 and 2:00–2:13) to see strokes and the
cursor in motion. Sizes below are converted to the 1600×900 `khan` board (video frame width 1003 px → ×1.6).
Narration is paraphrased, never transcribed.

## 2. Cross-video patterns

1. **What appears.** Handwritten words and short phrases (every video, 10–40 per video), equations (V1, V3: 4–8
   lines), numbers/digits (V2, V5), arrows (short, hand-drawn, ~1 per 30 s; curved as often as straight; also used
   as bullets ↑↓), underlines and circles (~1 per minute; always right after the thing is written or when it is
   mentioned), braces, boxes around a term (V4), tables without lines (V5), graphs with axes (V1 ×2, V3, V5),
   tiny pictures (V2, V6: 1–2 per minute). Pasted images are rare but real (V1 calculator, V6 paintings/quote).
2. **How it appears.** Everything handwritten is drawn stroke by stroke in real time while he talks; nothing
   handwritten ever appears whole. Pasted images appear whole. Rates: a 5-letter word 1.5–5 s (fast when it is a
   label, slow when he is thinking); a short equation line 8–15 s; axes + 2 ticks + 2 labels ≈ 13 s; a curve one
   sweep 1.5–3 s; a tiny picture 3–6 s. Writing roughly keeps pace with speech (KA's own guideline) — then a long
   gap: typically 30–50 % of each video has NO ink being added; he talks and points.
3. **Handwriting.** Quick print with cursive joins in frequent letter groups ("lim", "dem", "quant", "Reactions");
   5–10° right slant; baselines drift ±5 px; letter sizes vary ±20 % within a word; mixed case (capitals only for
   mnemonics/abbreviations). No size hierarchy: titles are body-size and get an underline or colour instead.
   Body cap height ≈ 45–50 px on a 1600 board (x-height ≈ 28–32 px); 2006–07 videos are 2× larger still.
4. **Colours.** 5–8 per video. A new noun/idea gets a new colour (V1: function yellow, evaluation coral, graph
   pink, hole/line green, limit blue). Annotation/answer uses a colour that contrasts with the thing annotated
   (V2 answers pink, V1 "undefined" coral). Within one equation, factors are colour-coded by origin (V3). White
   is for neutral asides and axes (V5), or for the last of several items when the palette is used up (V2, V5
   row A). Yellow is the default/opening colour in 5 of 6 videos.
5. **Layout.** Start top-left with the topic word or first equation; proceed left→right, then a new row 1/4–1/3 of
   the board down; annotations are squeezed into the gaps beside what they annotate; graphs go bottom-left or
   bottom-right. Irregular spacing; 70–100 % of the board filled before scrolling (V4 twice, V3 pan) or wiping
   (V1, V2, V3 at a new example). The board is never cleaned "for neatness".
6. **Pictures.** Crude and tiny: circle + squiggle = rock on grass; box + line + hatching = block on surface;
   circle + rays = sun/planet; circle with a face = a thing with a mood; circles joined by lines = particle path;
   stacked words with + between them = a reaction. Always labelled with one word and often an arrow. Drawn in the
   sentence that introduces them.
7. **Graphs.** Axes = two single strokes; arrowheads on both ends (V1) or far ends (V5) drawn as separate tiny
   strokes; ticks and labels only at the integers he will use (2–5 per axis), written one at a time; axis names
   at the ends ("x", "y=f(x)") or rotated along the axis ("Price"). Curves in one sweep; points as filled dots in
   the series colour, labelled beside; holes as open circles; converging small arrows show "approaching".
8. **Movement.** Between writes the cursor goes to the thing being talked about and hovers there, drifting
   1–3 px, for 2–20 s (V1 0:28–0:46). It travels visibly (≈ 0.3–0.5 s) from the end of one stroke to the start of
   the next; it never jumps. It rests on the last written thing while the sentence finishes.
9. **Pen.** ≈ 3 px at 1600 wide (2011), 1–2 px aliased (2006), 4–5 px (2007 zoomed); mild thick/thin from
   pressure; smooth; round ends. Re-tracing a stroke to thicken it is his emphasis (V2 double underline, V3
   underline).
10. **Khan-feel extras.** Talk before the first stroke (3–60 s, median ≈ 15 s). Words abbreviated when long
    ("quant. dem."). Headings underlined with a wavy stroke straight after writing. Coefficients/insertions added
    into an already-written line (V4 5:28). Groups bracketed with a hand brace. Flubs are left in.

## 3. What `khan` the software does today vs Sal

Sources read: spec.md §4.4 and §6, SKILL.md §4 and §6, `player/renderer/core/*.js` and `pictures/*.js` headers,
`shared/layout-core/constants.js` and `tokens.js`, `player/engine/scheduler.js`.

- Sal: a glyph is one or two pen strokes along its centre-line | khan today: the font OUTLINE is traced
  (`drawable.js` 'glyph': outlined while in progress, filled when complete) so letters look traced then "pop" solid
  | change: reveal glyphs by a left-to-right clip of the filled glyph (or a single-stroke/skeleton font) so ink
  appears where the pen is.
- Sal: writes at hand speed, then pauses and points (30–50 % of the time no ink) | khan today: each element is
  stretched to fill its whole window (`scheduler.js` drawEnd = start + max(window, naturalMs)) → slow, continuous,
  machine-even writing | change: draw at natural speed (≤ ~1.5× stretch), then park the pen on the element.
- Sal: pen ≈ 400–600 board px/s for handwriting, a 5-letter word ≈ 2–5 s | khan today: `PEN_PX_PER_S` 1500 |
  change: ~550 px/s plus per-word (120 ms) and per-line (400 ms) pauses in naturalMs.
- Sal: first stroke after 3–60 s of talk (median ≈ 15 s) | khan today: first element at `at` 0 (`defaultAts`)
  | change: default first `at` ≈ 0.08 (≥ 1.5 s), or the skill sets it.
- Sal: cap height ≈ 45–50 px, titles same size + underline | khan today: body 28 px, note 22 px, title 44 px
  (`FONT_SIZES`) → writing ~40 % smaller than Sal's, title bigger than Sal's | change: body ≈ 38 px, note ≈ 28 px,
  title ≈ 44 px with an automatic underline; retune `textWordsPerCell` (10 → 6).
- Sal: slanted, joined, uneven letters | khan today: upright, even Patrick Hand outlines | change: 6° shear +
  per-glyph jitter (baseline ±2 px, size ±4 %, rotation ±2°) at stroke-build time; audition Kalam.
- Sal: math is handwritten (fraction bar is a stroke, tall parens, small exponents) and colour-coded per factor
  | khan today: MathJax TeX-font glyph outlines (`math.js`, `svg-strokes.js`) → typeset look; one colour per element
  | change: keep MathJax for positions, substitute handwriting-font glyphs for letters/digits/operators; allow a
  restricted `\color{accentN}{…}`.
- Sal: axes with arrowheads, 2–5 integer ticks with labels, names at the ends, dots on points, holes as open
  circles | khan today: `plot.js` axes = rough lines + end ticks + min/max labels, polyline series, label top-right
  | change: arrowheads, integer ticks drawn after the axes, axis names at ends, `points` series drawn as dots with
  labels, `hole` option.
- Sal: tables have no grid; underlined headers, a colour per row | khan today: `table.js` draws muted grid lines
  first | change: `lines: false` default style = underlined header only; optional `rowColors`.
- Sal: pictures are 60–120 px, 2–5 strokes, with a word label and an arrow | khan today: `sketch` fills its slot
  (≥ 250×200 cell) and the library is infra-flavoured (database, server, cloud) | change: `size: small` (≈ 40 % of
  the cell, label beside), add rock/ball, block-on-surface, sun, smiley, particle path, brace, reaction stack.
- Sal: emphasis = wavy underline right after a heading, double underline, box around a term, circle around a
  letter, brace grouping | khan today: `highlight` circle/underline/strike/pointer (`highlight.js`) | change:
  wavy underline, `double`, `box`, `brace` styles; the skill underlines every title.
- Sal: cursor hovers over the thing being discussed for 2–20 s, drifting; travels visibly between strokes | khan
  today: pen follows the tip, parks only on `pointer` highlights, idles only when stalled (`pen.js`,
  `engine/index.js`) | change: after an element completes, hover over it with 1–3 px drift until the next start;
  tween the tip to the next start over 300–500 ms.
- Sal: lists are plain lines, indentation or ↑↓ arrows, no bullets | khan today: `list.js` rough bullet or number
  per item | change: `marker: none|dash|arrow` with `none` default for unordered lists.
- Sal: board fills then SCROLLS (V4) or pans (V3) | khan today: `wipe`/`region` only (spec §4.4) | change: a
  `scroll` board mode that shifts everything up by N rows and frees them (big).
- Sal: pastes a photo/screenshot and annotates it | khan today: no images by design (spec §2) | change: none now;
  note for a later `image` element with an allowlist.
- Sal: 5–8 colours, white for asides | khan today: 5 accents + muted, chalk reserved for neutral text (SKILL §4)
  | change: none — the rule already matches; add KA purple `#A87DFF` as `accent6` if the token contract is reopened.
